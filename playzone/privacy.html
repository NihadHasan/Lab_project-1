Privacy Policy

PlayZone does not require a player account. If you submit a score, the game name, score, optional display name and submission time are stored in the site database and may appear on the public leaderboard. Your best score is also stored in this browser. Game starts are counted to provide basic play statistics.

Administrator sessions use a necessary session cookie. PlayZone does not currently integrate an advertising provider; game recommendations link to PlayZone's own games.

Contact us at hello@example.com with privacy questions. Review and adapt this informational page before public launch.
